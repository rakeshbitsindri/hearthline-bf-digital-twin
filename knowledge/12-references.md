# Sources

What each reference is good for, and whether you can actually read it. Prefer the open ISIJ / J-STAGE papers and the IEA PCI review when a claim needs a primary source. IspatGuru is useful for orientation and is not peer-reviewed.

## Books

| Work | Use it for | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Geerdes, Chaigneau, Lingiardi, Molenaar, van Opbergen, Sha, Warren. *Modern Blast Furnace Ironmaking: An Introduction*. 4th ed., IOS Press, 2020. ISBN 978-1-64368-123-8. | Practitioner standard: thermal control, gas flow, burden distribution, casthouse, balances. 4th ed. rewrites the balance chapters. Kurunov and Ricketts contributed to the 3rd edition (2015), not the 4th. | [IOS Press](https://www.iospress.com/catalog/books/modern-blast-furnace-ironmaking-1). Front matter free at [ebooks.iospress.nl](https://ebooks.iospress.nl/publication/56020). |
| Peacey and Davenport, with Hopkins. *The Iron Blast Furnace: Theory and Practice*. Pergamon, 1979. | Canonical Rist model, stoichiometry, enthalpy, tuyere injectants. Appendix I is the reference derivation for flame temperature. | Elsevier reprint. |
| Cameron, Sukhram, Lefebvre, Davenport. *Blast Furnace Ironmaking: Analysis, Control, and Optimization*. Elsevier, 2019. ISBN 978-0-12-814227-1. | Modern successor. First-principles balances built in a spreadsheet, with operating data from five continents. | [Elsevier](https://shop.elsevier.com/books/blast-furnace-ironmaking/cameron/978-0-12-814227-1). |
| Biswas. *Principles of Blast Furnace Ironmaking*. Cootha, 1981. | Physical chemistry, reduction thermodynamics and kinetics. | [CiNii BA01422623](http://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA01422623). |
| AIST. *The Making, Shaping and Treating of Steel*, 11th ed., Ironmaking volume. | Design, coke, ores, operating rules of thumb. | [AIST / ASM](https://www.asminternational.org/hts/results/-/journal_content/56/05161G/PUBLICATION/). |
| Omori (ed.). *Blast Furnace Phenomena and Modelling*. Elsevier Applied Science, 1987. | Quenched-furnace dissections: cohesive zone, deadman, raceway, alkali circulation. Still the main source of internal-state ground truth. | Library. |

## Journals and conferences that actually contain the cases

| Source | Use it for | Access |
|---|---|---|
| ISIJ International and Tetsu-to-Hagané | Raceway geometry, flooding, cohesive zone, thermal reserve zone, high-reactivity coke, hearth drainage. | Open on [J-STAGE](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/browse/isijinternational). |
| Ironmaking & Steelmaking | European practice, coke quality, high PCI, stave cooling. | Subscription. |
| Steel Research International | Slag viscosity, bell-less top DEM. | Subscription. |
| Metallurgical and Materials Transactions B | Raceway momentum, hearth wear, transport. | Subscription. |
| AISTech proceedings | North American practice and plant ML papers. | [aist.org](https://www.aist.org/). Some digital-transformation papers are free PDFs. |
| ESTAD and METEC | European plant practice, hydrogen trials. | Proceedings. |
| SAIMM Furnace Tapping | Taphole, clay, drill, wear. Best open casthouse source. | Free PDFs, e.g. [Geyer](http://www.saimm.co.za/Conferences/FurnaceTapping/097-Geyer.pdf). |
| Molten Slags, Fluxes and Salts | Slag viscosity, optical basicity, sulphur capacity. | [pyrometallurgy.co.za](https://www.pyrometallurgy.co.za/). |
| ABM ironmaking seminars | Brazilian plant cases: Ternium staves, CSN scaffolds, Gerdau tapholes, hydrogen balance. | [abmproceedings.com.br](https://abmproceedings.com.br/). |

## Open reviews worth downloading first

| Document | Why |
|---|---|
| [IEA CCC/116, *Use of PCI in blast furnaces*](https://www.sustainable-carbon.org/wp-content/uploads/dlm_uploads/reports/Combustion/use-of-pci-in-blast-furnaces-ccc-116.pdf) | Replacement ratio, burnout, lance design, unburnt char. |
| [Bailera et al., Revisiting the Rist diagram, Open Research Europe 2021](https://doi.org/10.12688/openreseurope.14275.1) | Full Rist coefficients, including hydrogen. [PMC10445826](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10445826/). |
| [ACS Omega survey of data-driven soft sensing in ironmaking](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11191081/) | Names distribution shift as the core obstacle. |
| [JFE Technical Report 26, ventilating anomaly detection](https://www.jfe-steel.co.jp/en/research/report/026/pdf/026-05.pdf) | PCA Q-statistic on shaft pressure. |
| [Nippon Steel Technical Report 123-15](https://www.nipponsteel.com/en/tech/report/pdf/123-15.pdf) | Stave imaging, scaffold thickness, cohesive-zone root. |
| [Tata Steel soft sensors, ISIJ Int. 2022-246](https://doi.org/10.2355/isijinternational.isijint-2022-246) | Burden descent index, rainy-season pellet case, hearth remnant thickness. |
